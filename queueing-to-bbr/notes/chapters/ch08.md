## Spine and strands
Spine: the cover letter's 24 Mbit/s flow whose RTT drops from 2.195 ms to 0.165 ms (§9). Decomposed: two TSQ skbs of 2 segments, 3,028 wire bytes each at 24 Mbit/s = 1.009 ms per gap, 2.02 ms + path; goodput 24 × 2896/3028 = 22.95 vs the letter's 22.9. Capstone: "TCP writes its departure time into skb->tstamp; fq honours it."
Strands (= h3 ids = quiz lists):
- `s-afap` — AFAP: the queue decides when
- `s-tcp-stamps-departure` — TCP writes the departure time
- `s-rtt-from-departure` — The RTT clock starts at departure
- `s-fq-honours-stamp` — fq honours the stamp
- `s-so-txtime` — Any socket can stamp, on fq's clock
- `s-etf-launch-time` — ETF: a launch time that can be missed

## Measured on the bench
Rootless qnet, kernel 7.2.6-arch2-1, iproute2 7.2.0, 2026-10-02. Data and setups: `labs/ch08-edt/data/` (README there). Nothing needed sudo.
- TCP replay, 0.03 ms path, `tcp_tso_rtt_log=0`, SO_MAX_PACING_RATE 24 Mbit/s: `fq maxrate 24mbit` (v7.2 still gives EDT skbs a wire-bytes-from-now gap, sch_fq.c:796–833) = the 2018 "before": rtt 2.031/0.01, median 2.026 ms, unacked 4, pairs 1008 µs, 22.955 Mbit/s. Plain fq = "after": 0.028 ms, `cwnd:20 ssthresh:20 … unacked:4`, 964 µs, 24.001. fq_codel (internal timer): 0.016 ms, unacked 0, 24.001. No socket cap under maxrate: 7-segment skbs, 7.077 ms (check question 3).
- Sweep, 14 caps 6–192 Mbit/s: rate-model RTT = 2 × segs × 1514 × 8 / cap, measured 4–18 µs above it (sawtooth: 34 Mbit/s 1.431 ms, 36 Mbit/s 2.026); EDT 0.023–0.060 ms throughout. Plot `notes/plots/ch08-ratemodel.svg`.
- UDP SO_TXTIME (CLOCK_MONOTONIC) through fq, 1,000 stamps 1 ms apart: median 13.2 µs late (p99 24.9, max 122); groups of 8 handed down reversed leave in stamp order; stamps 45 ms past leave at once; 11 s ahead: `horizon_drops 20`; `horizon 2s horizon_cap`, 5 s ahead: `horizon_caps 20`, left 0.9 µs apart. Re-checked after the coordinator's bench correction (r1 as qnet built it, 40 ms on c0): median 12.9 µs, RTT 40.16 ms.
- ETF (`clockid CLOCK_TAI`) attaches rootless on veth. Releases each packet delta early plus ~3 µs (−497 µs at 500 µs, −17 µs at 20 µs); delta 10 µs: 30 of 1000 `TXTIME_MISSED` (5 in an earlier run); delta 0: dropped 1000, overlimits 1000. Past stamps, a CLOCK_MONOTONIC socket and ping: dropped at enqueue, `ENOBUFS`, `TXTIME_INVALID_PARAM` on the error queue for SO_TXTIME sockets. Drops ARP (needs a permanent neighbour) and the kernel's own IPv6 packets (6 in 8 s). `clockid CLOCK_MONOTONIC`: "Invalid clockid. CLOCK_TAI must be used."; `replace`: "Change operation not supported by specified qdisc."
- CAP rule: TAI socket works as userns root; with `setpriv --inh-caps=-net_admin --bounding-set=-net_admin` TAI gives EPERM, MONOTONIC works.
- CLOCK_TAI == CLOCK_REALTIME on this laptop (systemd-timesyncd leaves the TAI offset 0).
- Open, not in the page: in reversed-stamp UDP runs the first ~10 packets left up to ~0.6 ms late; a startup effect, mechanism not chased.
