Inventory corrections:
- Q3: "drops at 0, 100, 170.7, 228.4 …" is right for the formula, but "t_k ≈ 2I√k" is the large-k shape only:
  t_33 is 994 ms, 2I√33 = 1149 ms. The exact sum is I(2√(k−1) − 1.46 + …). The page uses both, labelled.
- Linux does not follow the formula exactly in the first gaps: rec_inv_sqrt gets one Newton step per drop
  (codel_impl.h:80–90), so the gaps are 100, 50, 56.25, 48.8 … ms (measured on the bench). After re-entry
  from a distant count (300 → 15) the first gaps are 6–10 ms instead of 25.8 (lines 242–246 say so).
- `codel_impl.h:93` is the comment line; the function is 97–102. `codel_should_drop()` is 104–144 as stated.
- The brief's command `tc -n rtr qdisc replace dev r1 root handle 1: tbf …` fails on the qnet bench
  ("Invalid qdisc name: must match existing qdisc"): qnet's root is netem with handle 1:. Delete first, or use
  another handle. Replacing a child with a different kind under the same handle fails the same way.

## Measured on the bench
All rootless, kernel 7.2.6, 2 October 2026; data and commands in `labs/ch03-codel/data/README.md`.
- pfifo limit 1000 under a 10 Mbit/s tbf, one Reno flow: 9.54 Mbit/s, TCP RTT median 655 ms (p95 1237),
  backlog median 513, cwnd median 540 (window − pipe = 507), ping median 653 ms.
- codel: 8.35 Mbit/s, RTT median 42.2 ms (p95 49.8), backlog median 1 (p95 8), ping max 52 ms;
  36 drops in 35 s (Mathis predicts one per 0.85 s; measured 0.97 s).
- fq_codel: same TCP numbers; ping median 40.4 ms, max 41.3.
- codel, four Reno flows: 9.40 Mbit/s total, Jain 0.9996. fq_codel with ECN: 26 marks, 0 drops, 0 retransmits.
- Step 10 → 1 Mbit/s at 20 s: pfifo RTT 6.5 s (530 packets × 12.1 ms); codel spike 360 ms, count 29,
  under 100 ms within 2 s, then ldelay ≈ 45 ms, ping ≈ 65 ms (MTU floor at 1 Mbit/s).
- UDP 11 Mbit/s into codel: first episode 3.207 s, count 286 at exit (model 285 at 3207 ms); count 34 at 1.0 s;
  later episodes resume at 285, 15, 164, 20 …; 84 drops/s over 10 s against an 83/s excess.
