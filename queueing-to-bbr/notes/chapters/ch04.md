## Spine and strands
Spine: one 10 Mbit/s uplink shared by two customers, A and B. A TBF (or HTB's root class) caps the uplink; HTB gives each customer 5 Mbit/s guaranteed and lets it borrow up to 10 when the other is idle; inside a class, DRR shares among flows.
("Customers", following the HTB user guide, instead of "tenants": "tenant" is a planned term of the pods chapter.)

Strands (h3 titles in brackets):
1. token-bucket-bound (The token-bucket bound) — bytes in any interval t are at most b + r·t (sch_tbf.c:36; Le Boudec Def. 1.2.1 p. 7); burst, limit = rate·latency + burst.
2. shaper-vs-policer (Shaping delays, policing drops) — shaping delays, policing drops (Le Boudec p. 10); a shaper is a queue (2.46 s RTT through the default leaf queue).
3. htb-borrowing (Rate, ceil and borrowing) — rate guarantee, ceil cap, tokens/ctokens, three modes, lended/borrowed, spare split by quantum.
4. drr-fairness (Fair in bytes at constant cost) — Max/Min unfairness of packet round robin (p. 233), deficit counter, Theorem 4.2 (p. 236), O(1) iff Quantum ≥ Max (p. 237).
5. max-min-fairness (Max-min fairness and its ideal) — water-filling (B&G pp. 527–528), GPS/BR as the fluid ideal, WFQ and DRR as approximations, Jain's index as a measure.
6. work-conserving (Idle on purpose) — DRR, HTB with ceil above rate vs TBF and ceil = rate; the watchdog timer (sch_tbf.c:322).

## Errata and since-the-paper notes
- Erratum (DRR SIGCOMM p. 237, verified on the rendered page): "We compare the FairnessIndex and Work … in Figure 1" means Table 1 (Figure 1 is the parking lot). The ToN version says "Table I" (p. 380).
- Man-page slip (tc-tbf PARAMETERS, verified by measurement): `latency` is described as "the maximum amount of time a packet can sit in the TBF", but tc sets limit = rate × latency + burst (95268 = 1.25e6 × 0.05 + 32768), so under sustained load a packet can wait limit/rate = latency + burst/rate = 76 ms. Measured: 75 ms of backlog, RTT 115.5 ms on a 40 ms path. The kernel comment's formula (sch_tbf.c:69) is the burst-into-a-full-bucket case.
- Since the man pages: tokens are nanoseconds of sending time (sch_tbf.c:295, 306; HTB reports them in 64 ns ticks, pkt_sched.h:48–50) and the watchdog is an hrtimer (sch_api.c:633), so the HZ = 100 limits in tbf ALGORITHM/PARAMETERS and htb NOTES no longer apply: HTB's default 1600-byte burst reached 9.56 of 9.56 Mbit/s here.
- Since the man pages: quantum = rate / r2q is clamped to 1000…200000 bytes when not given (sch_htb.c:2033–2042); the man page omits the clamp. The LINK SHARING ALGORITHM section is still "FIXME"; the code calls HTB "another implementation of Floyd's formal sharing" (sch_htb.c:46), i.e. Floyd & Jacobson 1995.
- Since the paper: ToN 1996 replaces the fairness index with an interval measure, FM(t1, t2) ≤ 2Max + Q (Theorem 3, p. 379). Linux sch_drr (2008) checks size ≤ deficit like Fig. 4 (sch_drr.c:389) with default quantum = MTU + link header (sch_drr.c:86). HTB's DRR sends first and charges after, so its deficit may go negative (sch_htb.c:926–928). fq_codel is DRR over hashed flows (RFC 8290 §1, sch_fq_codel.c:300–319).
