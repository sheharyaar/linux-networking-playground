## Inventory

- Tenants share per flow under TCP (p. 297).
- The hose constraint is in footnote 1 (p. 299). Admissible traffic is defined on p. 300.
- Fig. 2 (pp. 299–300): the UDP tenant sends 10 Gb/s for 5 ms and idles 15 ms, averaging 2.5 Gb/s; the TCP tenant's latency rises more than 10×; with EyeQ it is about 55 µs.
- §2.3 (p. 300): congestion stays at the edge under three conditions.
- Detect at the receiver, enforce at the sender (p. 301, Fig. 5). A 1 MB buffer fills in 800 µs.
- The meter runs every 200 µs. HACK packets are 64 B per 10 kB, at most 64 Mb/s (p. 302).
- RCP rule R ← R(1 − α(y − C)/C). It is the logistic map with b = 1 + α (footnote 2), stable for 0 < α < 2; the authors use 0.5 (pp. 302–303).
- About 30 iterations, 6 ms (p. 303).
- The sender module sits in the hypervisor vswitch (§4, p. 303).
- Per-CPU token buckets with a 50 µs timer (p. 304).
- Fig. 6: about 5 ms against 14 UDP senders (p. 305).
- Seawall and Oktopus are too slow, at 2 s (p. 308).

## Measured on the bench
Rootless qnet, kernel 7.2.6-arch2-1, 2 October 2026. Data and setups are in `labs/ch12-pods/data/` (README there).

**Stamps across rtr** (`cross-*`). 1,000 UDP datagrams, 1 ms apart, handed down 5 ms early; s0 on noqueue, plain fq on r1. Median lateness at r1:

| sender | lateness at r1 |
|---|---|
| no stamp | −4,935 µs |
| CLOCK_MONOTONIC | +12.4 µs |
| CLOCK_TAI | +12.7 µs |
| CLOCK_REALTIME | −4,931 µs |

At r0 every run was −4,935 to −4,939 µs. A burst of 50 monotonic stamps arrived 4.5 µs apart at r0 and left 1,000.1 µs apart at r1. fq on r1 reported `throttled 2050`, which is 1000 + 1000 + 50.

**EDT overload** (`edt-*`). podedt.py at 10M, which is 1,250,000 B/s or 1,211.2 µs per 1,514 B:

| run | delivered (s 4–7) | delay | other |
|---|---|---|---|
| under (8 Mbit/s offered) | 8.0 Mbit/s | 0 ms | |
| over (20 Mbit/s, fq on r1, flow_limit 100, 2 s horizon) | 0.59–0.62 Mbit/s | 1,999.7 ms | flows_plimit 7,354; offered 13,211, stamped 8,256, horizon drops 4,954, 903 delivered |
| bigfq (flow_limit 2000) | 10.0 Mbit/s | 2 s | |
| short (100 ms horizon) | 10.0 Mbit/s | 100 ms | |
| kept (fq on s0, SO_SNDBUF 212,992) | 10.0 Mbit/s | 85.8 ms | sender blocked: offered 6,673 |
| keptbig (8 MB) | 0.58–0.63 Mbit/s | 2 s | flows_plimit 7,354 |

The over run matches Little's law: 100 packets / 2 s = 50 pkt/s ≈ 0.6 Mbit/s. The plot is `notes/plots/ch12-overload.svg`.

**Rate loop** (`eyeq-loop.txt`). With N = 14 starting from line rate, α = 0.5 and a 1 Mb/s floor, the rule takes 31 steps (6.2 ms) to come within 0.01%. The linear part alone takes 13.3 steps. α = 2.1 never settles.

**System card.** `unshare -Urn cat /proc/sys/net/core/default_qdisc` returns "No such file", which is the kind limitation shown without a cluster.

- Since-note shipped: in 5.18 only TCP set the bit, and UDP wrote the stamp bare (v5.18 ip_output.c:1550).
