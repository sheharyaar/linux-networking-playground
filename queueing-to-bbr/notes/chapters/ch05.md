## Inventory
**TSQ article.** §1 bufferbloat = excess buffering anywhere in the stack. §2 CoDel and BQL exist; BQL only at device level; value in limiting above it. §3 TSQ limits data queued per socket "regardless of where the data is queued"; sysctl `tcp_limit_output_bytes`. §4 default 128 KB. §5 uses `sk_wmem_alloc`; `tcp_write_xmit()` stops, marks throttled. §6 refill when a queued skb is freed: overridden destructor; cannot send from it (locks), so a tasklet. §7 Dumazet quote: no longer 3 MB backlogged in pfifo_fast by one netperf session; autotuning no longer 4 MB. §8 full wire speed at 10 Gbit/s with a small limit. §9 Herbert's questions (complex qdiscs; tie to BQL); pulled for 3.6.
**TSO/FQ article.** §2 TSO defined. §3 TSO bursts for slow flows. §4 autosizing: 64 KB → ~1 ms at the current rate. §5 pacing defined ("rate ... doesn't cause them to pile up"; can also cap a flow). §6 patch computes the rate only; FQ enforces. §7 packet schedulers; FQ for local flows, also implements pacing. §8 per-flow state, 8-bit hash into an array of rb-trees, quota and next transmit time. §9 delayed flows in a second rb-tree keyed by time; single timer. §10 new and old lists; new served first; favours short flows. §11 per-flow and global limits; "internal" high-priority queue. §12 GC: detached flows freed after 3 s. §13 merge expected soon, perhaps after 3.12 (it went into 3.12-rc1).

## Spine and strands
Spine: the TSQ article's netperf session with 3 MB in pfifo_fast on its own sender (§7); reproduced on the bench as 2.3–14.9 MB with netem orphaning, and cut to 4–7 packets by TSQ under tbf.
Strands (= h3 ids = quiz lists):
- `tsq-limits-bytes-below-the-socket` — A limit on bytes below the socket (BQL merged in)
- `tso-autosize` — One skb per millisecond
- `pacing-rate` — A rate TCP computes and someone else enforces
- `fq-per-flow-scheduling` — One flow per socket, served in turns
- `fq-throttled-tree` — Waiting flows in a tree of times

## Measured on the bench
Rootless qnet, kernel 7.2.6-arch2-1, 2026-10-02. Data: `labs/ch05-small-queues/data/` (README there).
- tbf 10 Mbit/s on `s0` + pfifo 10000, delay moved to rtr: backlog 4–7 pkts (median 9,084 B), wmem_alloc median 14,448 B (two 3-segment skbs; pacing 40.6 Mbit/s at 200% because ssthresh never set), RTT 48 ms on a 40 ms path, 9.52 Mbit/s, 0 retrans, ~540 KB notsent. Same with lob 64 KB; lob 4 KB → median 4,542 B, RTT 44 ms.
- netem rate 10mbit limit 10000 on `s0`: wmem_alloc 0 throughout, backlog median 6.5 MB, max 14.9 MB, RTT 1.5–8.1 s.
- TSO autosizing with fq + SO_MAX_PACING_RATE 20/50/100/200/500 Mbit/s: bursts 2/4/8/16/42 segments, gaps 1157/923/920/913/938 us (predicted 1158/927/927/927/973).
- fq + 5 Mbit/s cap: pairs every 4,632 us (predicted 4,634), 5.001 Mbit/s payload, 5.229 frames; `throttled 2303` for 2,314 skbs; latency 127 us. pfifo + cap: identical (internal pacing). Uncapped steady state: pairs 2.42 ms apart in both; fq `fastpath 4453 throttled 282`. Slow start: pfifo 16 pkts in 40–50 ms then silence; fq pairs 3–6 ms apart.
- Two fq flows (2 and 5 Mbit/s): `flows 2 (inactive 0 throttled 2) ... next_packet_delay 1.07ms`, `throttled 862 latency 11.3us`; `flow_limit 2` dropped nothing.
- tcpdump runs rootless inside the userns netns (no `-Z` needed; there is no tcpdump user here).
- Open puzzle, not in the page: with netem orphaning, wmem_queued reached 17 MB against a 4 MB sndbuf; after each writer wake-up (at wq ≈ 2/3 sndbuf) the application wrote ~3× sndbuf in one 10 ms sample. Reproduced with sndbuf 1 MB (3 MB per wake). Mechanism not chased; the page says only that the queue follows what the application writes.
